I am an astrophysicist specializing in the X-ray coronae of active galactic 
nuclei (AGN), coronal line emission, and multi-wavelength quasar properties. 
Starting September 2026, I will be a postdoctoral researcher in the 
Department of Astronomy at the University of Geneva, working with 
Prof. Claudio Ricci.

I completed my PhD in astrophysics at the University of Science and 
Technology of China (USTC), supervised by Prof. Jun-Xian Wang, and spent 
a visiting year at Durham University working with Chris Done and Martin Ward.

My research centers on Comptonization physics, coronal temperature and 
optical depth, X-ray occultation events, time-lag analysis, and 
multi-wavelength characterization of AGN.
